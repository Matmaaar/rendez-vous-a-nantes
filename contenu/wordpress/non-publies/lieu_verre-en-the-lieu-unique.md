# The Lieu Unique

*Rubrique : Prendre un verre. Non publié sur le site actuel.*

> Between the SNCF train station La Cité Nantes Congress Centre

These converted former premises of the LU biscuit factory, the Lieu Unique is an atypical arts centre that houses a restaurant, a bar, a bookshop and a hammam. It is an area of artistic exploration, cultural excitement and conviviality that blends genres, cultures and audiences.
