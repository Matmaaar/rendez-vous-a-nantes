# The Hangar à Bananes

*Rubrique : Prendre un verre. Non publié sur le site actuel.*

> With its many bars and terraced-seating overlooking the Loire

This banana warehouse built in 1950, 150 m long and 50 m wide, to ripen the fruit as it arrived from Guinea and Guadeloupe. It now includes an exhibition hall, restaurants and bars whose outside seating looks directly over the Loire.
