# Absence

*Rubrique : Prendre un verre. Non publié sur le site actuel.*

> At the foot of the School of Architecture, on the banks of the Loire

Absence is a piece of artwork by Atelier Van Lieshout (AVL). The sculpture with its multiple lumps, bumps and protrusions appears to be a moving, living mass. It is also a bar and a great spot to go for a drink at the foot of the school of architecture on the banks of the Loire.
