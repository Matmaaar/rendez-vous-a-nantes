# Le Voyage à Nantes, the summer event

*Rubrique : Pass Nantes (24h de +). Non publié sur le site actuel.*

### Save the date ! Edition 2023 : from 1st July to 3rd Septembre 2023 [Program online](https://www.calameo.com/read/000106866bc1745281adc)

Every summer since 2012, the Voyage à Nantes Event makes a feature of the town by bringing together a multitude of cultural events. Its strength lies in the diversity and the free admission to most of the programme!
For two months, art infuses throughout Nantes and weaves social collusion. Artists, architects, designers and gardeners revive the 22 kilometres of the trail. The doors of cultural spots open wide to welcome large numbers of the public 7 days a week. A constellation of artists unfurls in the public space which in turn becomes places to meet and greet. Artworks popping up on the street corners!
