# Follow the Green Line

Let yourself be carried along to the rhythm of the city, lose yourself like a real Nantais …

Nothing could be simpler, just follow the green line drawn on the ground. Ideal for discovering the essence of the city. This 22-kilometre trail brings Nantes’ cultural sights together, its major monuments, works of art and must-see sites.
