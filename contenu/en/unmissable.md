# The unmissables

**10 GOOD REASONS TO TAKE THE VOYAGE AMONG TEN THOUSANDS OF OTHERS….**

**AND LEAVE A LITTLE ROOM FOR THE UNEXPECTED**

It couldn’t be easier to see everything you’d want to in Nantes and not miss a thing. In town, just follow [the green line](https://www.nantes-tourisme.com/en/le-voyage-nantes-trail) drawn on the ground. A 22-kilometer route that connects the cultural sights, the best monuments, works of art and tourist spots.

**PASS NANTES : EXPLORE THE CITY FREELY !**

The [Pass Nantes](https://www.nantes-tourisme.com/en/visit/nantes-pass) allows you to discover the town with complete peace of mind. Pick from 24h, 48h, 72h or 7 days passes and gain access to more than 50 services including public transport (including airport shuttle), excursions, museums, unmissable sites, tours, river cruises along the Erdre, etc.

## Liste

1. [The Château des Ducs de Bretagne](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/the-chateau-des-ducs-de-bretagne/) — The Nantes History Museum
2. [The musée d’arts – Arts Museum](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/the-musee-darts-arts-museum/) — 12,000 artworks from Ancient to Contemporary Art.
3. [Bouffay district](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/bouffay/) — The medieval quarter
4. [Le Lieu Unique](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/1279/) — The former LU biscuit factory - Central station district
5. [The botanical garden](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/le-jardin-des-plantes-en/) — Three centuries of botanical adventures
6. [Passage Pommeraye](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/le-passage-pommeraye-en/) — A 19th century covered alleyway
7. [The Machines de l’Île](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/the-machines-de-lile/) — The Machines Gallery, the Carrousel of the Marine Worlds, The Grand Elephant.
8. [The Creative Quarter, l’Île de Nantes](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/the-creative-quarter-lile-de-nantes/) — Modern Architecture, the Machines de l’île and the banks of the Loire
9. [Chantenay district](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/quartier-chantenay/) — Panoramic view of the Loire
10. [Memorial to the abolition of slavery](https://rdv.levoyageanantes.fr/en/unmissable/le-memorial-de-labolition-de-lesclavage/) — Contemplative walkway unique in Europe
