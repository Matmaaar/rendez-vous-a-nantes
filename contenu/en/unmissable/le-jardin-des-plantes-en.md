# The botanical garden

> Three centuries of botanical adventures

With its 7 hectares (17 acres) of greenery right in the heart of the city, over 10,000 living species, 800 m2 (8,600 sq. ft.) of greenhouses, Nantes’ botanical garden is considered to be one of the four most beautiful in France.
