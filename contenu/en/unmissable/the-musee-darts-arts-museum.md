# The musée d’arts – Arts Museum

> 12,000 artworks from Ancient to Contemporary Art.

After 7 years of renovation, the Musée d’Arts in Nantes re-opened in 2017 with a new 2,000 m² building dedicated to contemporary art. Artists include; De La Tour, Ingres, Ernst, Delaunay, Chaissac and Picasso…. The collection ranges from ancient to contemporary art.
