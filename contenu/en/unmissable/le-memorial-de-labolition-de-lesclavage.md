# Memorial to the abolition of slavery

> Contemplative walkway unique in Europe

Unique in Europe, this contemplative space, spread above and below this stretch of road along the Loire, presents quotations in every language,from every historical period, and every country. Its 2,000 commemorative plaques are a reminder of every slave ship that ever left Nantes

## Infos pratiques

- **Adresse** : Near the Passerelle Victor-Schoelcher, Quai de la Fosse, 44000 Nantes
