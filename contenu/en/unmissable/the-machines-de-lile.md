# The Machines de l’Île

> The Machines Gallery, the Carrousel of the Marine Worlds, The Grand Elephant.

The emblematic Machines de l’Île are at the top of everyone’s ‘to do’ list when they come to discover Nantes. The 12m high Elephant, the 25m Carousel dedicated to the marine world, etc.
