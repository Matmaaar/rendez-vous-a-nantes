# Bouffay district

> The medieval quarter

The medieval district of Nantes. In addition to the half-timbered houses and remains of fifteenth and sixteenth century buildings, this district is full of small shops, bars and restaurants in all shapes and styles. There is a constant buzz in this lively part of town and an incomparable atmosphere.
