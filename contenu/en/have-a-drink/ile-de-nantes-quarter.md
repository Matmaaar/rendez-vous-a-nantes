# Ile de Nantes district

Along the Loire, near the Buren Rings is the Hangar à Bananes, an ideal spot for a drink outsite while listening to a band play. Plenty of  bars and atmospheres  in this rehabilitated industrial building.

**Unmissable places:**

- **The Cantine du Voyage (7D/7D From 18 April to 6 October 2024** **),** with its table football and deckchairs facing the Loire!
- **L’Absence,** the bar of the School of Architecture, an artworks of Atelier Van Lieshout, a Dutch group of designers

Late night and buzzing atmosphere.
Some are open until 5.30 am.

**A few suggestions :**Address : Quai des Antilles

- Le Ferrailleur
- Le Point Bar
- Le SAO
- La Calle
- Canaille Café
