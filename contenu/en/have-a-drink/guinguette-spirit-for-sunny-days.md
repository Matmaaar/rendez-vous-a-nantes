# Guinguette spirit for sunny days

> On a sunny day

In and around Nantes, a few temporary and friendly guinguettes are open to locals and visitors.

No better place for getting some fresh air while in holiday mode than a riverside tavern in the town.

A few suggestions :

- La Cantine du Voyage *– Until 6 October 2024*
- La Station Nuage *– Reopening spring 2025*
- La Guinguette du Belvédère – *Reopening spring 2025*
- Le Bateau Lavoir – *Reopening spring 2025*
