# Getting around

[airport shuttle](https://rdv.nantes-tourisme.com/en/getting-around/#navette) · [public transport](https://rdv.nantes-tourisme.com/en/getting-around/#transports) · [taxis](https://rdv.nantes-tourisme.com/en/getting-around/#taxis)

## Airport shuttle 7d/7d (except on 1st May)

**>> Included in Pass Nantes :** **1 round trip included during the period of validity of the pass. Compulsory validation of the journey on the Pass Nantes app.**

Shuttle to and from Nantes’ airport: direct and quick service for all your trips.
Every day of the week, only 20 min long, the shuttle is the quickest way to connect the airport to the town centre.

**Timetable:**

- **Every 20 minutes from Monday to Saturday.** From the station from 05:30 to 22:30. From the airport 06:15 to 23:15.
- **Every 30 minutes Sundays and public holidays.** From the station from 06 :10 to 22:40. From the airport 07:00 to 23:30.
- **Fares:** 10€ a ticket, lasts for an hour on the entire public transport network (NAOLIB).

- Contact : [+33 2 40 44 44 44](tel:+33240444444)

## Public transport TAN (Greater Nantes Transport).

**>>Included in Pass Nantes : your Pass Nantes is valid on all public transport in Nantes (airport shuttle included). No extra ticket is needed. It may also be used on the river ferries Navibus.**

The public transport system is managed by NAOLIB (Greater Nantes Transport).
A multi-means network: **3 tramway lines** **(1, 2 and 3)** which criss-cross the very heart of the town centre.

Take Line 1 from the town centre to Beaujoire Stadium or ExpoNantes (+/- 25 minutes)

[**Route Maps and Trip Calculator**](https://plan-tan.fr)

**Please contact this European emergency number.**
