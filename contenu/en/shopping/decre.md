# Bouffay distric also known as Decré district

> Galeries Lafayette and independent shops

Situated in the heart of the medieval district, in a maze of cobblestone streets, the Decré district is full of boutiques, major retailers, independent shops, home furnishings.

Rue de la Marne, rue des Chapeliers, rue du Chateau, rue des Carmes, rue de la Paix ….
