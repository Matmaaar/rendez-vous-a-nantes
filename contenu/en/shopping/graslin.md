# Graslin district

> Rue Crébillon, Passage Pommeraye

The Graslin district is famous for its chic stores, its gourmet restaurants, the Pommeraye shopping arcade – one of the most beautiful covered arcades in Europe – and its shopping streets (including rue Crébillon, rue Santeuil, rue Grétry).
