# Talensac Market

> An institution in town

Celebrated for the diversity of its products and the quality of its artisans, it is the oldest and most important market in Nantes. Open every morning of the week except Mondays.

## Infos pratiques

- **Adresse** : Rue de Talensac 44000 Nantes
