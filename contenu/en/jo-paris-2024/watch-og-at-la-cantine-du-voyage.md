# Watch OG at La Cantine du Voyage

### **The Cantine du Voyage is an original pop-up venue where locals like to hang out and an essential stop on any visitor’s Voyage à Nantes itinerary.**

### **The Cantine and the OG Paris 2024:**

### **Opening days and times:**

- Open from Wednesday to Sunday
- Opening times: From 11 am until late (2 am in the morning)
- Matches shown on big screens.

### **Brochettes and snacking boards on the menu !**

- **Brochette meal deal:**
  Set menu including a meat, fish or vegetarian (falafel) brochette as a main course.
  Served with seasonal veg and new potatoes.
  Set menu includes: 1 starter salad + 1 brochette with veg & potatoes + 1 drink
  Adult menu price: €15 or €17.50 with a dessert.
  Child’s menu price: €10 (includes a brochette of their choice + 1 dessert + 1 drink).
- **Large snacking boards** to share during the game (including mini brochettes, charcuterie, cheese, spreads and breads, etc.).
- **Drinks bar and dessert bar** open all day long (pastries, ice cream by the scoop).

### **Getting there**

**On foot from the *Club 2024* :** 25-minute walk
**With public transport**:

- Tramway Line 1: Chantiers Navals station (then a 15-minute walk)
- Chronobus Line C5: Quai des Antilles stop (just behind the Cantine)
- Navibus Line N2: River bus between Bas-Chantenay and Hangar à Bananes (runs every 20 minutes, crossing time of 6 minutes).
