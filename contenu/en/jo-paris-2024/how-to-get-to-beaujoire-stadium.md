# How to get to the Beaujoire Stadium ?

### **IMPORTANT – ORGANISERS’ RECOMMANDATIONS :****Allow plenty of time to get the stadium.****Leave town three hours before the start of the match at La Beaujoire.**Example : **for a match at 5pm (E**gypt – Dominican Republic) **–> set off from Nantes at 2pm.****Entertainment and food will be available around the stadium entrance.**

### **RECOMMANDATIONS WITH PUBLIC TRANSPORT : FREE TRAIN & TRAM**

- **TRAIN – From Nantes Train Station
  Gare Nord Side access. No need to enter the hall**// **Suggested itineraries :** **10 minutes ride****No ticket required, free from Nantes
  Go to stadium : from 12pm –> train every 15 minutes.
  Back from stadium : during 3 hours after the match –> train every 15 minutes.**From Nantes train station (Gare SNCF) :
  – Take the train TER/Aléop –> direction Châteaubriant **OR** Nort sur Erdre **OR** Haluchère **OR** Sucé sur Erdre
  – Get down at the « Haluchère-Batignolles-Beaujoire » stop. (only 1 stop after Nantes)
  – And then walk for 9 minute (1000 meters). Parcours signalé à l’arrivée.
- **TRAMWAY – LINE n° 1 – Beaujoire OR Ranzay
  // Other** **itinerarie :** **30 minutes ride** **from city centre****Public transport is free on Saturdays and Sundays \***– Take the Tramway Line n°1 – direction Beaujoire **OR** Ranzay
  – Get down at the Beaujoire stop
  – And then walk for 6 minute (500 meters).  **\*** Download the TAN (Nantes transport network) application to calculate your journey.
  [Download the TAN app for Apple](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/tan/id502653639)
  [Download the TAN app for Android](https://apps.apple.com/fr/app/tan/id502653639)

- **By bike:**Download the [GEOVELO](https://geovelo.app/fr/) app to identify the best route according to your point of departure.

- **Parking at the Beaujoire Stadium:**The stadium offers car parks including two car parks for people with reduced mobility (PRM).
  For more details, see the FAQ section.
