# Nantes – Club 2024

### **An area to celebrate the OG PAris 2024** **in the heart of the Feydeau district****Open from 26 July to 11 August included : Free entry****District Feydeau – Close to the Castle of the Dukes of Brittany.**[Itinerary](https://www.google.com/maps/place/Feydball/@47.2143653,-1.5725567,15z/data=!4m19!1m12!4m11!1m3!2m2!1d-1.5502836!2d47.2144964!1m6!1m2!1s0x4805eea56e6afabd:0x164b796dbd073eaa!2sCr+Franklin+Roosevelt,+44000+Nantes!2m2!1d-1.5514393!2d47.2143948!3m5!1s0x4805eea56e6afabd:0x164b796dbd073eaa!8m2!3d47.2143948!4d-1.5514393!16s%2Fg%2F11cs63c80x?entry=ttu)  Carré Feydeau

The *Club 2024* is a meeting and gathering place, open to all. Olympic events broadcast on giant screens, huge interactive street art mural, local farmers’ market, pre-match supporter clapping, programme of live music open to local performers, brass band performances, and more.

### **Activities t****hroughout t****he day :**

- Animated demonstrations and activities throughout the visit

### **Openind days & Opening times :**

- Every day from 26 July to 11 August included
- From midday to midnight

### **How to get there ?**

**With public transport:**

- Tramway Line 1: “Bouffay” station.
- Tramway Line 2 or Line 3 : “Commerce” station and 2 minutes on foot.

**By bike:**
180 bike parking spaces available at the *Club 2024* grounds.
Self-service city bike are also available : [**Naolib bike**](https://velo.naolib.fr/en/tutorial)

### **Going on to the Beaujoire Stadium from the *Club 2024* ?**

Take the “tram-train” to the Haluchère Batignolles-Stade Beaujoire station (direct train with seating, 10-minute journey time).
OR
Tramway Line 1 from Station Chantiers Navals to Station Beaujoire.

### **Frequently Asked Questions:**

**Is it possible to come to the club with a dog?** No, except for service animals.
**Can I bring alcohol and food?** No.
**What payment methods are accepted in the Club 2024 ?** Credit card or cash.
**Can I enter the village on a scooter?** No.
**Are rucksacks and suitcases allowed? If so, of what size?**Bags must not exceed a capacity of 10L to be allowed into the village.
**Can I smoke in the Club 2024.** Yes, but with respect for other spectators.
**Are there toilets in the Club 2024 ?** Yes.
