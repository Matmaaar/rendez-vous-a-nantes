# Frequently Asked Questions – Beaujoire Stadium

## Entering the stadium:

**Must I carry ID to enter the stadium?**Yes. You must be able to show official photo identification upon request from an authorised Organising Committee member or the police, in the case of identity checks on ticket holders.

**Can you bring alcohol or other beverages or food** **into the stadium?****Are prohibited :** liquids of any kind, including alcoholic or non-alcoholic beverages, in bottles, cans, re-usable bottles or flasks.
**Are authorized :**– re-usable bottles (plastic or metal) of maximum than 75cl
– plastic bottles of maximum than 75cl
– baby bottles
– medical prescriptions
– food permitted in small quantities for personal consumption.

## Vehicles/Parking:

**Which are the car parks for persons of reduced mobility near the stadium?**
The PRM **car parks « S4 »** will be available for visitors (40 spaces)
Presentation of disability card required.
Address : 5 Route de Saint-Joseph, 44300 Nantes.
Guarded parking (CCTV and security guard).

**Where to park around the stadium?**
**Free and unrestricted access :**
Parking lots S1 – S2 – S4 PMR – Roseraie – Ranzay
**Free parking 3 hours before the match and 2 hours after (H-3 ; H+2) :**
P+R Beaujoire – Ranzay – Haluchère 1 and 2

**Can visitors leave their vehicle in a TAN P+R car park for several days?**No, it is not possible to leave your vehicle in a P+R car park for several days.

**Where can I park for several days?**
In a **“Parking Relais P+R Voyage”.** (park-and-ride facility)
– Parking relais « Prairie de Mauves »
– Parking relais « Neustrie.P+ »
More info [**Parkings Relais NAOLIB**](https://naolib.fr/fr/voitures-et-deux-roues/parkings-relais-pr)

**Where can I park my bike or scooter at the stadium?**

2 000 spaces are available for bicycles : “Parking S2”.

The helmet must be chained to the bike with the anti-theft device.
Guarded parking (security guard)

**Can motorbikes/mopeds park beside the stadium?**A motorbike parking facilities are available in front of the entrance N°1 of the Exhibition Centre (Parc des expositions).
Address : Route de Saint-Joseph de Porterie, 44300 Nantes.
Limited number of spaces

**Are there special parking rates for match ticket holders?**No.

**Is it possible to park a bus beside the stadium?
Is there a bus drop-off ?**A Car Park will be available on « Terrace P » of the Exhibition Centre – Parc des espositions.
Adresse : Next to the stadium

**Is there a special NAOLIB ticket price (Public transport of Nantes) for match ticket holders?**Public transport NAOLIB is free on Saturdays and Sundays.
Free on all match days (From Monday to Friday, 3h before and 2h after the match)
Bus, Navibus, Tram in city center and Nantes Métropole.

**Is there a special TER train ticket price for match ticket holders?**/ Journeys between Nantes and La Chapelle-Aulnay are free at weekends.
/ The Pays de la Loire Region and SNCF-TER are offering tickets at a price of €5 per journey, when travelling second class from a station in Pays de la Loire to the Haluchère-Batignolles-Beaujoire station and vice versa.

## Personal Belongings:

**Who should I contact regarding lost property at the stadium?**To the “Info point” agents and then at the Consigne No. 1, at the main ticket desk.From 09/08, items will be transferred to the Nantes Metropole « lost property department ».

**Can I come to the stadium with a pushchair?**Yes. Special spaces will be welcome them on the inner forecourt.

**Where can I leave my luggage in the vicinity of the stadium?****NO LOCKERS – No cloakroom.**
Do not bring luggage.

**Is it possible to come with a rucksack? If so, of what size?**Maximum bag size allowed: 25cm x 25cm x 25cm, or a maximum volume of 25 litres.

## Miscellaneous:

**Which streets will be closed? Will residents be informed?**Some streets will be closed, like for major events at the Exhibition Centre and the Stadium. Residents will be notified this summer and will need to collect a badge from their neighbourhood team to park in their street.
