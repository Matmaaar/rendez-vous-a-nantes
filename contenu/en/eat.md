# Eat

> Looking for a great place to eat?

**Les Tables de Nantes guide book** lists over one hundred tested and approved addresses. Using this well thought out search engine, you will surely find the right place for you: eating with colleagues, vegetarian, big groups, quirky, out-door seating, in the heart of town, etc… For all tastes and budgets.

## Some ideas for Monday evenings dinner

- [Maison Bagarre](https://www.lestablesdenantes.fr/en/restaurants/maison-bagarre/)
  :
  *In his cozy bistro, Romain Eveno prepares dishes with sensational seasonings, using quality seasonal and local ingredients.*
- [Roza](https://www.lestablesdenantes.fr/en/restaurants/roza)
  :
  *Sophisticated, intuitive and delicate cuisine, served in a stylish setting (bearing the signature of Brune de la Guerrande), that will surely impress.*
- [Crêperie HEB KEN](https://www.lestablesdenantes.fr/en/restaurants/creperie-heb-ken/)
  :
  *These brave restaurateurs have been ardent defenders of the Breton crêpe for 40 years.*
- [Balthazar](https://www.lestablesdenantes.fr/en/restaurants/balthazar/)
  :
   *Enjoy sublime meats grilled to perfection, and other delicacies cooked marvellously. And, lest we forget the fish chowder!*
- [La Cigale](https://www.lestablesdenantes.fr/en/restaurants/brasserie-la-cigale)
  :
  *Sought out for its sumptuous and protected heritage interior, this brasserie was once the headquarters for the Surrealists, and still never disappoints.*
- [Little Atlantique Brewery - LAB](https://www.levoyageanantes.fr/en/places/little-atlantique-brewery/)
  :
  *Craft micro-brewery in Bas Chantenay by the river Loire in a refurbished 19th industrial building.*
